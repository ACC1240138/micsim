# ENPG raw-data audit for the EPS extension

1. **The feasible beverage split is most frequent beverage, not any beer/wine/spirits consumption.** In every audited wave, the beverage question identifies a single most frequently consumed beverage in the last 30 days. It does not identify all beverages consumed, beverage-specific grams, or beverage-specific HED. Report preferred-beverage shares among current drinkers and overall HED stratified by that preference. Keep other/unknown responses visible. Do not allocate all consumption to the preferred beverage.

2. **ENPG does not cover the requested older groups.** The nine raw sources cover ages 12–65, except the observed 2010 maximum is 64. There are no observations over 65. The extension includes 15–17 as a separate supplemental group and uses 18–29, 30–44, 45–59, 60–65 for comparison with EPS. The 66–70, 71–76 and 77+ rows remain NA with an explicit unsupported-age flag. ENPG consists of repeated cross-sections and cannot identify individual persistence or rho.

3. **Two distinct HED instruments must remain distinct.** From 2012, a direct count asks about occasions in the last 30 days with at least five drinks for men or four for women, all beverages combined. Count zero means no HED; 88/99 mean unknown. In 2018, raw labels use 888/999, while the questionnaire says 88/99 and four observations actually contain 88/99: flag this ambiguity. AUDIT asks usual frequency of six or more drinks, uses a different threshold, and does not establish a last-30-day event. AUDIT never is 0 and positive frequency is 1:4, except 2018 where never is 1 and positive is 2:5. The 2010 p021 label only says frequency of consumption; its threshold remains unverified without the original questionnaire.

4. **Skips are part of the outcome definition.** Ever-use no identifies current use=0. Last-use recency 2 or 3 identifies current use=0; current use=1 requires recency 1. Unknown ever-use/recency remains NA. Direct HED is a structural zero for known non-current users and an item missing value for current users with unknown HED. Report both HED prevalence in the age-sex population and HED among current drinkers. The estimate excludes unknown outcomes; coverage and missing-outcome bounds use the full relevant domain. A positive frequency cannot be inferred from missing AUDIT values.

5. **Question wording introduces comparability limits.** The 2016 and 2018 questionnaires exclude Fiestas Patrias. The 2020 and 2022 instruments conditionally exclude New Year for January 2021/2023 interviews. The 2024 instrument conditionally excludes Fiestas Patrias and New Year. The female four-drink examples in 2020, 2022 and 2024 repeat the male five-drink volumes (1,600 mL beer, 700 mL wine, 200 mL spirits); earlier instruments use 1,300/550/160 mL. This is a documented questionnaire inconsistency, not something recoverable from the responses. ENPG 2020 also used pandemic-era fieldwork adaptations. None of these instruments supplies an exact pure-ethanol HED threshold without additional beverage-strength assumptions.

6. **Latest-wave design and data checks pass.** ENPG 2024 has 18,668 records, 17,748 age18+, weights summing to 11,396,772, 2,692 UPM and 109 ESTRATO. All design fields are complete; weights are positive; no UPM crosses strata; the full sample has at least five UPM per stratum. Full design is retained before forming domains. Small age-sex/beverage domains do contain singleton strata; working confidence intervals use the notebook's explicit domain adjustment and expose their number. The older waves do not all expose equivalent design identifiers; the crosswalk distinguishes published fields from unvalidated candidates/proxy strata.

7. **2024 outcome checks and results.** There are 5,533 current users, 143 unknown current-use responses, 2,327 observed zero-HED responses, 2,857 positive-HED responses and 349 unknown HED responses among current users. Preferred beverage is unknown for 24 current users. Among ages 60–65, estimated current prevalence is 33.0% for men and 17.8% for women; HED prevalence in the whole age-sex population is 12.3% and 5.25%; among current drinkers it is 37.6% and 31.2%. These are survey-weighted complete-response estimates with separate coverage, interval and missing-outcome bounds, not official published SENDA estimates. Direct HED is labelled SENDA 5+/4+; do not silently rename it the Calvo threshold.

8. **Raw values require care in historical work.** The 2012 direct HED count contains seven implausible values from 500 to 10,000, which must be flagged before estimating historical HED. Counts above 30 are not automatically invalid because the item counts occasions, not drinking days. The latest 2024 source has two responses of 40 occasions, retained as positive HED. Raw-label and questionnaire audits precede any historical recoding. The 2016 public survey file has no expansion factor; its separate weight file must be joined using idencuesta, with one-to-one and completeness checks.
